# City of Dasha 🏝️💖

A 3D island game designed by Sasha.

**Play it here: https://chen168.github.io/city-of-dasha/**

Play as Dasha, collect things around your island, craft decorations, make friends who move in,
play 15 games at the Game Fence, go fishing at the dock, go to school (and lunch!) with your friends,
mine gems in the cave, shop at the 4-floor mall (charms, surprise draws, a diner and an arcade), visit
your friends' houses, decorate your own house, adopt a pet, grow a garden, paint floors, ride a scooter,
take photos, and buy clothes with Dasha coins. The island celebrates spring 🌸, Easter 🐣, Halloween 🎃
and winter ❄️.

Works on iPad, iPhone and computers. On iPad or iPhone, tap Share → *Add to Home Screen* to play it like an app.

This repository holds the built game: `index.html` contains all of the code.
